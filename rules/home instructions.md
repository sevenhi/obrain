## Rules
- Device mapping: One note per device; keep logic (Automations) distinct from state.
- Automation logic: Track `Trigger` → `Action` → `Condition`.
- Bidirectional: Device ↔ Automation links are mandatory.

## Observations
- [convention] Every device note needs `[status]` and `[location]`.
